About Us

Your trusted support partner — available 24/7 to resolve your issues quickly and professionally.

Who We Are

Parimatch Support is a dedicated customer support portal built to give users a single, reliable place to report problems and get fast assistance. We understand how frustrating it can be when a deposit does not reflect, a withdrawal gets stuck, a bonus does not appear, or your account is temporarily locked. That is exactly why we exist — to listen, document, and push your complaint to the right team until it is resolved.

Our support team consists of trained professionals who handle complaints with care, patience, and complete confidentiality. Every complaint submitted through our portal is treated with high priority, regardless of the issue size.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to make support accessible, transparent, and stress-free for every user. We believe that every problem deserves a proper answer, and every user deserves to be heard without long queues or repeated follow-ups. We work around the clock so that help is always one click away.

What We Help With

We provide assistance across all common account and payment issues, including:

  • Withdrawal Problems — stuck, delayed, or missing withdrawals
  • Deposit Problems — payments that did not reflect in the account
  • Bonus Problems — missing bonuses and wagering-related queries
  • Bank Statement Issues — statements and transaction record requests
  • Unlock Withdrawal — help with locked or restricted withdrawals
  • KYC Problems — document verification and account approval support
  • Live Chat — direct assistance for any other question

How It Works

Getting help through our portal takes only a few steps:

  • 1 Open the Home page and choose the category that matches your problem.
  • 2 Click Complain Now and fill in the details of your issue.
  • 3 Our team reviews your complaint and forwards it to the concerned department.
  • 4 You receive updates until your issue is fully resolved.

Why Choose Us

  • 24/7 Availability — our team never sleeps; support is always online
  • Fast Response — complaints are acknowledged and processed quickly
  • Complete Confidentiality — your data and documents are handled securely
  • Clear Communication — you always know the status of your complaint
  • Multiple Categories — dedicated paths for every type of problem

Our Commitment

We are committed to fair, honest, and professional support. We do not make false promises, and we do not close a complaint until a genuine effort has been made to resolve it. Your trust is the foundation of our service, and we work hard to protect it every single day.

Contact

Need help right now? Choose the Live Chat option from the Home page, or submit a complaint under the relevant category. Our team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — we are just a click away.
